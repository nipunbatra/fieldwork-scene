How this dataset was made

The initial student-application audit found capture-date discrepancies, repeated detections, inconsistent counters and a broken export action. These are observations from the original four-case review; this expanded browser uses frozen predictions and makes no changes to the student server.

Keep AI proposals separate from reviewed labels. For RF-DETR, correct full-field boundaries, agree on burnt/partial/unburnt criteria, resolve uncertainty, and hold out geographic/acquisition groups. Do not silently treat unresolved fields as background negatives.
